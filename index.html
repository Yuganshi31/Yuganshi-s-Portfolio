About

I'm a Game Designer and Developer with a background in 3D rigging, currently focused on creating interactive gameplay experiences and learning how ideas turn into playable systems. I enjoy working across both the creative and technical sides of game development, from designing mechanics and building prototypes to implementing gameplay in Unity, Unreal Engine, and Roblox Studio.

Alongside game development, I specialize in 3D character rigging and have experience creating biped and quadruped rigs in Autodesk Maya. My understanding of rigging helps me think about characters, movement, and interactions from both an artistic and technical perspective.

I've worked on a range of personal, academic, and professional projects, including gameplay prototypes, Roblox experiences, Unity games, and character rigging projects. I'm always experimenting with new mechanics, tools, and workflows while building a portfolio that reflects the kind of games I want to create.

Currently, I'm pursuing an M.A. in Game Design & Development and looking to grow as a game designer and developer while contributing to meaningful game projects.
